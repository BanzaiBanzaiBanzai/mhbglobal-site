Information and purpose

Your email address and message are needed to answer an enquiry. Name, company and requested service are optional. We also process your selected language and any design choice included in your message. For company representatives and general questions, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding to relevant business enquiries. Where you personally would be party to a contract, we process necessary information to take steps at your request before that contract. Providing information is voluntary, but we cannot reply without a contact route. Please avoid sensitive personal information. We do not use enquiries for automated marketing or automated decision-making.

Email and retention

We review enquiries monthly. Closed enquiries that have not resulted in a project are removed at the first monthly review after six months from the last meaningful contact, normally no later than seven months. Active matters are kept until they can be closed. Necessary customer, contract and accounting records are handled separately for their purpose and applicable obligations. Spam and irrelevant messages are removed within 30 days.

Your rights

Email kontakt@mhbglobal.se to request access, correction, erasure or, where applicable, restriction and portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. We normally respond within one month and may need to verify your identity proportionately. You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
