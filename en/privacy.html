Privacy information

MHB Global AB is responsible for personal data you provide when contacting MHB Global Sweden. Use our contact form for questions about your information.

Information and purpose

We use your name, email address, optional company name and message to answer your enquiry and prepare a proposal you request. The basis is taking steps before a contract at your request, or our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries. Your details are not used for automated marketing mailings.

Form service and retention

Formspree receives the enquiry and sends us an email notification. It also uses technical information, including IP addresses, for security and spam filtering. Its free archive gives us access to submissions for 30 days. Email copies are kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting business relationship, or meet legal obligations.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your information and, where applicable, restriction, portability or object to processing. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY). Use our contact form and state that your enquiry concerns personal data.

Providers and transfers

The website is delivered through Cloudflare. Enquiries are processed by Formspree and our email provider Microsoft. Formspree processes data in the United States and states that it relies on EU Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers as a data processor.
